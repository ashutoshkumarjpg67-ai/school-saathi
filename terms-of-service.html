1. What the service is

School Saathi is a multi-school app (a web app / PWA, also available as an Android app — the same web app in a Capacitor wrapper) for schools in Bihar, covering classes Nursery to 12th. A school uses it to keep its own records: students, attendance, fees and receipts, exams and marks, homework, notices, and teachers.

The service consists of the app itself and the server that stores and syncs the school's records (Supabase project bsjqeeeemwimzldrehpw). There is no separate website or landing page yet — the app runs in the browser, can be installed as a PWA, and is also packaged as an Android app.

These terms cover the use of the app by a school and by the people the school allows to use it (teachers, staff, students and parents). The companion Privacy Policy (privacy-policy.html) explains what data is collected and how it is handled. The two documents are meant to be read together and must agree with each other — if they ever disagree, tell us (§11) and we will fix it.

2. Who may use it

  • Schools. A school creates one school account (email + password) and uses the app to manage its own records. A school account is for that school only — schools cannot see or change each other's data.
  • School admin. The person who creates and controls the school account. The admin adds students and teachers, enters fees and marks, and controls who can log in on the school's devices.
  • Teachers. A teacher logs in on the school's device with the login the school gave them, and works only with the classes the school assigned to them.
  • Staff. Staff accounts (Principal, Accountant, Staff) are created by the school admin; a Principal sees the whole school, an Accountant sees fees only, and Staff see only their classes.
  • Students. A student logs in with the PIN the school set for them, and sees only their own attendance, fees, results and homework.
  • Parents / guardians. A parent logs in with their child's admission number and the PIN the school set, and sees only that child's attendance, fees, results and homework.
  • There is no "super admin" login screen in the app — platform administration is a server-side role only.

By creating a school account or logging in, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

3. Your account — your responsibility

  • Keep your login safe. The school admin is responsible for the school account's email and password, and for the teacher logins and student PINs the school hands out. Do not share the school password with people outside the school. Anyone who has the school's login can open the school's records on that device.
  • One school, one account. Each school account belongs to one school. Do not use a school account to keep another school's records.
  • Tell us if the login is misused. If you think someone else has your school's password or a teacher/student login, change the password / PIN in the app and contact us (§11).
  • Password rules in the app today: the minimum password length is 6 characters. Passwords and PINs are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text — but the hash is a single-iteration SHA-256, which is not strong protection on its own if it leaks (see Privacy Policy §8). Choose a password that is not easy to guess.
  • Devices: the app stores the school's data on the device where it is used (browser localStorage). The school is responsible for keeping its devices locked and for who can open them.

4. Acceptable use

Use the app only for running your school's records. In particular:

  • Do not enter false, misleading or unlawful information.
  • Do not try to open, change or delete another school's data, or to log in as another school, teacher or student.
  • Do not try to break, overload or misuse the service — including trying to get at other schools' records through the server, or trying to bypass the login.
  • Do not use the app to send spam, or to send messages to people who have not agreed to hear from the school.
  • Do not upload anything that you do not have the right to keep in the school's records (for example, a photo of a child whose parent/guardian has not agreed to it — see §5).
  • The app has no built-in way to report misuse by another user. If you see misuse, contact us (§11).

5. Data accuracy and children's records

  • The school is responsible for the accuracy of the records it enters: names, classes, fees, marks, attendance and the rest. The app shows what the school entered; it does not check whether it is true.
  • Children's records: the app holds minors' school records (Nursery to 12th). The school creates a child's record at admission and is responsible for having the parent/guardian's consent or authority to enter and keep that child's data. The app does not itself collect a parent's consent. (See Privacy Policy §11.)
  • Keep your own backups. The app can export a full backup (JSON) of the school's data. The school should keep its own backups somewhere safe. A backup file contains the whole school database — treat it like a paper register and do not forward it to strangers.

6. Availability — no uptime promise

  • The app is built to work offline first: the school can keep working without internet, and records sync to the server when the connection comes back (the app tries to sync about every 30 seconds while it is open).
  • We make no promise about uptime and no service-level promise (no SLA). The service may be unavailable sometimes — for example, during maintenance or if the server is down. We do not promise a fix time.
  • Sync needs an internet connection. If the device is offline, changes stay on the device until it is back online. Logging out does not force a final sync — unsynced changes on the device are not uploaded by logging out.
  • We may update the app. When a new version is available, the app checks for it and reloads once to the new version. We do not promise advance notice for every update.

8. Ending the service / deleting your account

  • The school can delete its account in the app (Settings → "Delete Account"). It asks for the account password and for the exact school name typed out. If the school is linked to the server, the app asks whether to delete from this device only or from the server too. "From the server too" runs a server function that wipes the school's server rows and deletes the login account — after that, the same email cannot log in again. "This device only" deletes everything for that school on this device; the server copy (if any) is not deleted, and logging in again with the same email brings a server-linked school back. A school that was never linked to the server is gone for good. (See Privacy Policy §10.) If the server function has not been deployed yet, the app says so honestly and only the device-only deletion runs.
  • Reset All Data deletes all school data on the device, with an option "Delete on server too" (which wipes the school's server rows). But the login account itself is never deleted by it, and the same email and password can log in again afterwards.
  • Ending the service by us: no provider-side suspension or termination process is defined in the app today. Whether and how the service may be suspended or ended for a school — and what notice is given — is a decision for the owner. These terms do not invent a suspension power.
