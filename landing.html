What is School Saathi?

School Saathi is a school management app for schools in Bihar. It keeps students, attendance, fees, exams, homework and notices in one place — for the school office, teachers, students and parents.

What you get

🎓 Students

Admissions, profiles, photos and printable ID cards.

✓ Attendance

Mark attendance class-wise every day, including holidays.

💰 Fees & receipts

Collect fees, print receipts with a QR code, and see pending dues.

📝 Exams & results

Create exams, enter marks and see results.

📚 Homework

Assign homework class-wise and track what is done.

📢 Notices

Publish notices the whole school can see.

📊 Reports

Download CSV reports for students, fees and attendance.

💾 Backup & sync

Download a full backup, restore it when needed, and sync to the server when online.

📶 Works offline

Install it as an app. It works without internet after the first load.

Who uses it

School admin

Logs in with email and password. Manages the whole school.

Teacher

Logs in with email and password. Sees only their own classes.

Student

Logs in with admission number and the PIN given by the school.

Parent

Logs in with their child's admission number and PIN to see attendance, fees and results.

How a school gets started

  1. Open the app and tap “Create a school account” on the login screen.
  2. Enter the school name, your name, email and password.
  3. Add classes and students, then start marking attendance and collecting fees.

A new school starts empty — no demo data is added.

Pricing

Pricing is decided with each school — there is no fixed price list here. After creating your school account, raise a support request from inside the app and we will talk about it.

Your data

Your school's data stays on your device and backs up to the server when you are online. One account per school.
