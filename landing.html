How it works — कैसे काम करता है

Three steps, and the school is running on School Saathi.

1

School signs up

Open the app and create a school account with the school name, your name, email and password. Each school gets its own account.

2

Add students

Add students class-wise (Nursery to Class 12). The school sets each student's admission number and PIN.

3

Attendance, fees, exams

Mark attendance daily, collect fees with receipts, create exams and share homework and notices.

Frequently asked questions — अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Answered only from what the app actually does today.

Is School Saathi free?

Pricing has not been decided yet, so we cannot say it is free or paid. The pricing section above will be updated once a decision is made.

Which classes does it support?

Nursery to Class 12. Students are added class-wise, and attendance, fees, exams and homework are all managed per class.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The app works offline — data stays on the school's device and backs up to the server when the device is online again.

How does a school sign up?

Open the app, choose "Create a school account" and fill in the school name, your name, email and password. Each school has its own account, and a new school starts empty.

How do students log in?

Students log in with their admission number and a PIN set by the school. If a student does not know the PIN, the school provides it. Teachers log in with an account created by the school admin.
